About

I'm a combined Bachelor's/Master's student in Computer Science (minor in Mathematics) at Johns Hopkins University. I work with Prof. Tianmin Shu in the Social Cognitive AI Lab, and with Prof. Philipp Koehn and Prof. Berrak Sisman at the Center for Language and Speech Processing.

My research studies how AI systems reason about other minds, both people and other agents, and how meaning carries across languages through speech.

Computational Cognitive Science Theory of Mind Embodied & Multimodal AI NLP, Speech & LLM Agents
