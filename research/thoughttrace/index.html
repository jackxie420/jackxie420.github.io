Overview

Conversational AI reaches billions of users, yet existing datasets capture only what people say, not what they think. ThoughtTrace is the first large-scale dataset that pairs real-world, multi-turn human–AI conversations with users' self-reported thoughts.

Thoughts come in two forms: reasons for sending a prompt and reactions to the assistant's response. They are distinct from messages, hard for frontier LLMs to infer, and useful for predicting user behavior and aligning models.

Findings

Finding 1

Thoughts are hard for LLMs to infer

GPT, Gemini, and Claude struggle to recover users' thoughts from context alone. On a 1–5 similarity scale, predicted thoughts score only 2.93 for reasons and 2.54 for reactions. Thoughts carry information that never appears in the messages.

Finding 2

Thoughts predict what users say next

Giving models the annotated thoughts raises next-message prediction similarity from 21.6 to 30.6 on average, a 41.7% relative gain.

With thoughtsHistory only
Finding 3

Thoughts improve model alignment

Training on thought-guided rewrites beats message-guided rewrites on Arena-Hard by 4.5 points, and the base model by 25.6 points (style-controlled win rate).
