How to play

Place a stone on an empty intersection. Connect five horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.

Black starts. This is freestyle Gomoku: a line of more than five also wins. Changing mode starts a fresh game.

Using a keyboard? Tab to the board, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter or Space to place a stone.
