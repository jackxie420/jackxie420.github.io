Data & methods

Real hand calculations

Equity is calculated from the 52-card deck and standard best-five-of-seven Hold’em rankings. Your cards, board, and opponent cards cannot overlap. Opponent ranges retain their combo weights after blockers. Exact results count every valid outcome; simulations sample legal deals and show an approximate 95% sampling margin.

Hand-ranking reference ↗

Traceable preflop data

Frequencies are copied without rounding from the MIT-licensed poker_solver blueprint: 169 classes, four decisions, and 20/40/100 bb stacks. The data records the original configuration and SHA-256 hashes. Upstream reports no final exploitability measurement; frequencies are model estimates, not verified commercial GTO charts.
